## What changed
- **Switching is instant.** The 3D view keeps the original and the enhanced model loaded, so *Original* and *Enhanced* swap at once instead of loading the model again.
- **Generated models are kept.** The last three models you generate are kept. Generating again with settings you've already used (the same model, parts and every setting) brings the kept one back at once instead of making it again. A model you open again shows the last one generated from it.
- **Enhance is now Generate.** The tab and its button say *Generate* (*Generate again* once there is a result). The *Original* / *Enhanced* switch is unchanged.
- Edges in the 3D view are worked out only when you turn them on, which makes large models show sooner.
