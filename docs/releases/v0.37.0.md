**Painting has moved to the Finish tab, with more settings:** a colour tolerance, the texture each material is painted from, the smallest colour kept, and a brightness, contrast and saturation adjust.

## What changed
- **Paint for colour printing (Finish tab).** The painted 3MF's settings now live in their own section of the Finish tab, beside making the model watertight. The Export tab, with the 3MF format chosen, shows the colours it will paint with and a **Paint settings** button that goes back to them.
- **Colour tolerance.** Suggested colours closer than the tolerance are merged into one, so two filaments that come out nearly the same shade (two greys, two reds) become one. *Colours* is then the most the print uses: with a tolerance, there can be fewer. 5 is a just-visible difference; 20 merges light and dark shades of one colour.
- **Smallest colour.** A colour covering less of the surface than this share is dropped, and its part of the model is painted in the nearest colour left.
- **Texture each material is painted from.** Each material can be painted from any of the model's textures instead of its colour texture, or not painted at all (it then takes filament 1).
- **Adjust the texture colours.** Brightness, contrast and saturation change the textures' colours before they are reduced. Game textures are often dark or dull, because the game's lighting brightens them; the palette and the paint both use the adjusted colours.
- **Show the colours on the model** now stays on while you move between tabs, until you switch it off or leave the model.
- The settings are kept between sessions; the texture choices are kept for each model.
- Command line: `--tolerance`, `--min-share`, `--brightness`, `--contrast`, `--saturation` and `--paint-texture MAT=TEXTURE` (repeatable; `MAT=` alone leaves that material unpainted).

## Windows
- **`DarkSTL_0.37.0_x64-setup.exe`** installs Dark STL for your account (no administrator rights). It isn't signed, so SmartScreen asks once: *More info > Run anyway*.
- **`DarkSTL_0.37.0_x64-portable.zip`** is the same app in a folder: unzip it anywhere and run `Dark STL.exe`.
