**Paint the Finished model and cut pieces**: a model made watertight or cut into pieces can now be exported as a painted 3MF, with its colours taken from the textured model it was made from.

## What changed
- **Painted 3MF for the Finished model and the pieces.** These have no textures of their own, so each part of their surface now takes the colour of the textured model's surface at the same place. The paint is still finer than the triangles, split where the colours change, down to the smallest feature you set. Choose *Finished* or *Pieces* on the Export tab, then the Painted 3MF format.
- **Every piece gets the same palette**, so the filament slots match from piece to piece. One `<name>_palette.png` is written in the pieces' folder.
- **Cut faces, joints and filled gaps take filament 1.** These surfaces don't lie along the textured model, so they have no texture colour to take. The back of a thin surface made solid takes the colour of its front, as the game draws it.
- **The feature size comes from the whole model**, so a piece is painted as finely as the model it came from.
- **Flat models**, such as a plate that has almost no height, now take the print height along their longest side.
- **Fixed:** making a model watertight could fail with an error when its holes were closed in more than one loop. Bloodborne's c1120 hit this.
- CLI: `--watertight` and `--cut` work with a painted `.3mf` output.

Checked on Bloodborne's c1120 made watertight (893,000 triangles): reading the colours took 12 s and painting took 50 s. Side by side with the textured model, the colours match.

## Windows
- **`DarkSTL_0.24.0_x64-setup.exe`** installs Dark STL for your account (no administrator rights). It isn't signed, so SmartScreen asks once: *More info > Run anyway*.
- **`DarkSTL_0.24.0_x64-portable.zip`** is the same app in a folder: unzip it anywhere and run `Dark STL.exe`.

## Other systems
Python 3.10 or newer. Download the wheel below, then:

```
pip install "dark_stl-0.24.0-py3-none-any.whl[gui,remesh,fast,ai]"
darkstl
```
