**The model page reads in the order the work happens, and hands models to Model Painter in one click.**

## What changed
- **Tabs in three groups.** The inspector's rail now groups its tabs as the work goes: **Model** (Configure, Model, Textures, Pose), **Edit** (Mesh, Displace, Finish, Cut) and **Export**. Each tab's name sits upright under its icon instead of running down the edge; when the window is too crowded for the panel, the rail keeps the icons only.
- **One place to choose the model.** The Show buttons under the 3D view (Original, Enhanced, Finished, Pieces) choose the model you're working on. Finish, Cut and Export no longer have their own copy of these buttons: each says *Working on: Enhanced · change*, and *change* takes you to the buttons under the view. Finish and Cut only show a choice of their own while the view shows a model they can't work on (Finish can't take the Finished model or the pieces; Cut can't take the pieces).
- **Explanations fold away once you know them.** The paragraph at the top of Mesh, Displace, Finish, Cut and Paint shows its first sentence; the rest stays open until you've run that tab once, then folds away so the buttons fit on a laptop screen. The ⓘ beside the heading opens it again.
- **Next steps under each result.** After a step, buttons take you on: Mesh → Displace; Displace → Export or Finish; Finish → Cut or Export; Cut → Export; Export → Open in Model Painter.
- **Messages no longer cover the buttons.** On a model's page the short messages show at the top right of the 3D view. The bar under the view stays one row: when there isn't room for every view button, shading, edges and the Front/Top views fold into one *View* menu.
- **More room for the 3D view.** In a window under 1440 px wide, the game's tree beside the menu folds when a model opens and comes back when you go back. If you open or fold it on a model's page yourself, that's remembered.
- **Displace names its columns** (Texture, Strength) over the per-material rows, and the **Export folder** shows its last two folders (the whole path in its tooltip, and when you click into it to change it).
- **Export for Model Painter.** One button on the Export tab writes the model shown as a GLB with its textures and materials, Y up, named `<model>_for_painter.glb`. After an export, **Open in Model Painter** (beside Show in folder) opens the file in [Model Painter](https://github.com/DrFlGd/Model-Painter), where painting and print finishing now live. Dark STL finds Model Painter where its installer or its portable zip put it; otherwise set where it is in **Settings › Model Painter**. Without it, the Export tab says how to get it, and the file is still saved.

Tested in Chromium at 1280 × 720 on synthetic models: the rail's groups, the single picker, explanations folding after a run, the Next strip, the toasts' place, the folded game tree, the preset's GLB, and Open in Model Painter starting a stand-in program with the file.
