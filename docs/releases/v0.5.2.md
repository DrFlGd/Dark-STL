Stop and the two Generate steps are now in plain sight: a Stop button sits in the status bar while anything runs, and the Generate tab has a button for step 1 on its own, so step 2 can be tried on the evened-out model as often as you like.

## What changed
- **Stop is always shown.** While a job can be stopped, the status bar shows a red **Stop** beside it, not only when the mouse is over it. Clicking the job still stops it too, and the Generate tab's own Stop button is red.
- **Step 1 and step 2 have their own buttons.** *Run step 1 only* evens out the vertices and shows that model, not displaced (its relief is 0; it can be exported like any enhanced model). The main button then reads *Displace (step 2)*: it starts from that mesh and only adds the texture detail, so step 2's settings can be changed and run again as often as you like without evening out again. When no step-1 mesh is kept for the settings chosen, the button reads *Generate (steps 1 + 2)* and does both. Step 1 says *Step 1 is done* with its vertex count while its mesh is kept, and its button then shows the evened-out mesh again.

## Windows
- **`DarkSTL_0.5.2_x64-setup.exe`** installs Dark STL for your account (no administrator rights). It isn't signed, so SmartScreen asks once: *More info > Run anyway*.
- **`DarkSTL_0.5.2_x64-portable.zip`** is the same app in a folder: unzip it anywhere and run `Dark STL.exe`.
