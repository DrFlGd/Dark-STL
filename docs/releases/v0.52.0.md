**Grand Theft Auto IV opens in Dark STL, with The Lost and Damned and The Ballad of Gay Tony.**

## What changed
- **GTA IV (PC) is read by the RAGE importer GTA V uses.** Add the game's install folder as it is (the one with `GTAIV.exe`). Dark STL reads the models (`.wdr`, `.wdd`, `.wft`) and their textures (`.wtd`) straight from the `.img` archives in `pc\models\cdimages` and `pc\data\maps`. Nothing in the game's folder changes.
- **The archive key comes from your own game.** The archives' tables are encrypted, and the key is in `GTAIV.exe`. Dark STL doesn't include it: the first time you add the folder it finds it in your executable (a few seconds) and keeps it in its settings folder (`keys\gta4.bin`).
- **Sorted and named as in the game.** The game's `.ide` files and its text (`american.gxt`) put vehicles under Cars, Motorcycles, Boats, Helicopters... with their names from the game. Characters are grouped by kind (young men, women, story characters...), weapons, props and map pieces by their archive.
- **The episodes come along.** In the Complete Edition, the models of The Lost and Damned (`TLAD`) and The Ballad of Gay Tony (`TBoGT`) are listed with the base game's, their groups named after the episode (Motorcycles (The Lost and Damned)).
- **Vehicles come out whole.** As in GTA V, the stored wheel is drawn on every wheel bone, the right-hand ones turned to face out, and doors and other pieces sit at their bones. Textures come from the vehicle's own dictionary, the parents the `.ide` files name (`txdp`), and `vehshare`.
- **Characters are outfits.** A ped's `.wdd` holds one model per component; each ped is listed as a configurable model with a slot per component, skinned to the skeleton in its `.wft`.
- **Collect and packages.** Collect copies a model, its texture dictionaries and its ped skeleton out of the archives (as the files OpenIV exports), plus the `.ide` files and the text. The collected copy opens and is named without the game and without the key.
- Files exported with OpenIV or SparkIV open too, without the game.

## Not done yet
GTA IV's animations (`.wad`) aren't read yet, so its characters can't be posed: they open in their bind pose. Vehicle paint, ped texture variations, weapon names (weapons keep their model names) and map placement aren't read. The console versions come later.

This has only been tested on synthetic files built byte by byte: a GTA IV folder with an executable holding a made-up key, encrypted and plain archives, a car, a ped with three components and its skeleton, a weapon, a prop with its own textures, the lists and text, and a Lost and Damned bike. Nothing has been checked against the real game yet, so the key search in a real `GTAIV.exe` is still to be tried.

## Windows
- **`DarkSTL_0.52.0_x64-setup.exe`** installs Dark STL for your account (no administrator rights). It isn't signed, so SmartScreen asks once: *More info > Run anyway*.
- **`DarkSTL_0.52.0_x64-portable.zip`** is the same app in a folder: unzip it anywhere and run `Dark STL.exe`.

## Other systems
Python 3.10 or newer. Download the wheel below, then:

pip install "dark_stl-0.52.0-py3-none-any.whl[gui,remesh,fast,ai]"
