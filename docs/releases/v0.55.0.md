**Finish, Cut and painted 3MF have moved to Model Painter.**

## What changed
- **One place for print finishing.** Making a model watertight, cutting it into pieces with joints, and painting it from its textures for a multi-colour printer are now done in [Model Painter](https://github.com/DrFlGd/Model-Painter), which has done all three since its 0.3.0. Dark STL's own Finish and Cut tabs and its painted 3MF export are gone, so the two apps no longer do the same jobs twice.
- **What to do now.** Open the model's **Export** tab and click **Export for Model Painter** (it's at the top of the tab). Then click **Open in Model Painter**: Model Painter opens the GLB with its textures, ready to make watertight, cut or paint. Dark STL finds Model Painter where its installer or portable zip put it, or where *Settings › General › Model Painter* says.
- **A shorter model page.** The Edit group is now Clean, Mesh, Displace. The Show buttons under the 3D view are Original and Enhanced. After Displace, *Next* goes to Export; after an export, *Next* goes to Open in Model Painter.
- **What stays.** Clean (hidden parts, thin sheets, picked faces, repairs, print height and placement), Mesh, Displace (with its thin-part protection), the Thickness view, saved variations, posing, and every other export: STL, OBJ, PLY and GLB of the Original or Enhanced model, and the GLB that keeps the skeleton in your pose.
- **Old settings still load.** Finish, Cut and paint settings saved by earlier versions (in the settings file, in saved variations and in the bulk *Enhance with the current settings* preset) are ignored, and left out the next time each is saved. A model page last left on Finish or Cut opens on Export, and a painted 3MF chosen as the export format goes back to STL.
- **Command line.** `--watertight`, `--close-gaps`, `--drop-loose`, `--keep-hollows`, `--sheet`, `--cut`, `--clearance` and the painted 3MF options (`--colours`, `--palette`, `--print-height`, `--feature`, `--cleanup`, `--tolerance`, `--min-share`, `--brightness`, `--contrast`, `--saturation`, `--paint-texture`) are gone. `-o model.3mf` stops with "Painted 3MF moved to Model Painter: export a .glb and open it there". `--clean height:150` (the Clean tab's print height) stays.
- **A smaller app.** Dark STL no longer needs the manifold3d package.

## Windows
- **`DarkSTL_0.55.0_x64-setup.exe`** installs Dark STL for your account (no administrator rights). It isn't signed, so SmartScreen asks once: *More info > Run anyway*.
- **`DarkSTL_0.55.0_x64-portable.zip`** is the same app in a folder: unzip it anywhere and run `Dark STL.exe`.
- For watertight models, cutting and colour painting, install [Model Painter](https://github.com/DrFlGd/Model-Painter/releases) too.

## Other systems
Python 3.10 or newer. Download the wheel below, then:

pip install "dark_stl-0.55.0-py3-none-any.whl[gui,remesh,fast,ai]"
darkstl
