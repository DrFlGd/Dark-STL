**Spore Wizard B walks without blowing up.**

## What changed
- **Animations made on the model itself.** Some models' skeletons keep their bones turned another way than the model's own (a skeleton kept from an earlier version of the model: Returnal's Spore Wizard B). Its walk and idle were measured from the skeleton's pose, so the model came out two to three times its size and tilted. Dark STL now sees which of the two an animation's keys were made on and measures from that one, so the Spore Wizard B walks and idles at its own size.
- Models whose skeleton and bones agree pose as before, and a model that shares a skeleton with others of other proportions still keeps its own.

Checked on Jerred's exported Spore Wizard B (both of its animations: the game only has these two, kept in an *Animations_Old* folder). Byte-built tests cover a skeleton turned another way and a model of other proportions.

## Windows
- **`DarkSTL_0.41.1_x64-setup.exe`** installs Dark STL for your account (no administrator rights). It isn't signed, so SmartScreen asks once: *More info > Run anyway*.
- **`DarkSTL_0.41.1_x64-portable.zip`** is the same app in a folder: unzip it anywhere and run `Dark STL.exe`.
