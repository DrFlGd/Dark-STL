**Collections, tags and stars across every game, related models, turning previews, and doing things to many models at once.**

## What changed
- **Collections.** Make lists of your own from any game ("To print", "Bosses of every game"): right-click a model for *Add to collection…*, or drag cards onto a collection in the menu. A collection's page lists its models in the order you drag them into, with its own search, sort and list view. *New collection…* is in the menu and on Home, which now shows your collections as tiles.
- **Tags and stars.** *Tag…* puts words of your own on models ("cloth", "needs supports"): they show on the cards, the search finds them like names, and searching every game offers them as filter chips. *Star* marks the ones to come back to; *Starred* in the menu lists them. Collections, tags and stars live in one file (`collections.json` in the settings' library folder), keyed by the model's place in its game, so they survive rescans, collecting, packages and removing and re-adding a folder.
- **Related models.** A card says "3 related" when other models go with it: the rest of a FromSoftware armour set (head, body, arms, legs of one number), a weapon's sheath or variant, its low-detail copy, a character's variants, a Resident Evil character's face, body and hair, and the configurable models it's part of. The details panel and the model page's Model tab list them, and *Combine with other models…* offers them first.
- **Previews that turn.** Hold the pointer over a card for a moment and its picture turns round the model (eight views, drawn in the background after the preview). Characters are now drawn standing in a resting frame of an idle animation when they have one, instead of in their bind pose (Settings › General › Previews, on by default). The toolbar has a slider for small, medium or large cards.
- **Choose several models.** Shift+click chooses a run of models, Ctrl+click adds or takes one off, Ctrl+A chooses them all and Esc lets them go. Right-click them (or use the command bar) to *Collect into the app*, *Export as GLB*, *Export packages*, *Enhance with the current settings* (the settings you last generated that game's models with), *Add to collection…*, *Tag…* or *Star*.
- **The Queue.** Those bulk actions run one after another while you go on working. The new Queue page in the menu shows each with its progress and a Stop, says what it did when it's done, and lists any model that couldn't be done with why. The running one is in the status bar as before.
- **Problems.** A game's page now lists what needs a look, each with what fixes it: a folder still packed (*Unpack…*) or gone (*Remove*), models that couldn't be read (*Export model files…*, to send them for a look), files gone since the scan (*Rescan*), models without textures and textures that weren't found (*Choose texture…*, *Find textures…*), and animations that couldn't be read. Home has a line for each game with any, in place of the old folder notes.
- **First start.** With no folders yet, Home shows a tile for each game Dark STL reads; click one for what folder to choose for it, with *Add this game's folder…*.
- **Settings in three pages:** General (exports, Model Painter, previews, appearance), Library (game folders, the app library, texture folders) and Game tools (Oodle, Unreal games, game archives).

Byte-built tests cover the collections file and its survival across rescans and restarts, each importer's related models, the queue (bulk export, packages, enhance, stopping), the turntable and posed previews, the problems list, and the pages (adding to a collection from the menu and by dragging, tags and stars, choosing several and exporting them through the queue, the first-start tiles and Settings' pages).

## Windows
- **`DarkSTL_0.48.0_x64-setup.exe`** installs Dark STL for your account (no administrator rights). It isn't signed, so SmartScreen asks once: *More info > Run anyway*.
- **`DarkSTL_0.48.0_x64-portable.zip`** is the same app in a folder: unzip it anywhere and run `Dark STL.exe`.
