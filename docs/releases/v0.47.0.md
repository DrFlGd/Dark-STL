**Pose any bone, keep the poses you make, and attach a weapon or shield to a hand.**

## What changed
- **Turn any bone.** The Pose tab lists the model's skeleton as a tree with a find box. Choose a bone and turn it about its own X, Y and Z axes with a slider each, or type the angle in degrees. The turn goes on top of the animation's frame, or on the bind pose when no animation is chosen, and the bone's children follow. This works on models that have no animations too, as long as they have a skeleton (FromSoftware and RE Engine characters and armour, Unreal models whose animations are found, and the packages made of them). Bones that move none of the model's vertices are behind *Every bone*, so the list stays short.
- **Turn bones in the 3D view.** With *Show bones in the 3D view* on, a small ball marks each bone's pivot: click one to choose that bone, then drag the coloured rings around it to turn it. The sliders follow.
- **Mirror left and right.** *Mirror to …* gives a bone's partner on the other side the same turn, mirrored. *Mirror left → right* and *Mirror right → left* do it for every pair at once. Bones pair up by their names (L_ and R_, _l and _r, Left and Right, and the like). *Reset bone* and *Reset all* take the turns away; *Back to the bind pose* clears the animation too.
- **Saved poses.** *Save pose* keeps the animation, the frame and every turned bone under a name, with the model in Dark STL's library. Saved poses are listed at the top of the Pose tab to apply, rename or delete, and are offered on every model of the game with the same skeleton. The bar under the 3D view names the pose shown, and an export's file name takes that name.
- **Attach a model to a bone.** *Attach a model…* finds a model of the same game and puts it on one of this model's bones or sockets: a sword in a hand, a shield on an arm. It follows every frame and every turned bone, and goes through Mesh, Displace, Finish, Cut and Export with the model. On the Pose tab, choose the bone or socket that holds it, move it along the bone's axes in millimetres, turn it and scale it. Up to 16 models can be attached to one, and they're kept with the model.
- **GLB keeps the skeleton.** A GLB export of the model as shown now carries its skeleton in the pose you made, with the mesh bound to it, so Blender opens it posed and rigged. An enhanced model's GLB carries the skeleton in its pose without the weights.

Tested on synthetic characters in Chromium: choosing a bone, turning it by typing an angle, saving the pose, opening the model again and applying it, and attaching a second model to the hand (the triangle count doubles and the pose stays). Engine tests check that a turn moves the right vertices and its children's, mirroring, saved poses offered to a second model with the same bones, an attached model following its bone through a frame change, and the GLB's skeleton.

**Collected before 0.47.0?** Packages of models that have no animations were made without their skeleton. Collect them again to turn their bones.
