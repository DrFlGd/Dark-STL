**Save an enhanced model as a variation, beside the model, and come back to it.**

## What changed
- The **Displace** tab has a **Saved variations** section. After a Generate, give the enhanced model a name and **Save as variation**. It is kept with the settings, parts, options and pose it was made with, and a picture of it.
- Each saved variation is listed with its picture, when it was made, its vertex count and its relief:
  - **Show** brings it back as the enhanced model, even after other Generates or a restart. Export, Finish and Cut then work on it like a fresh one.
  - **Use its settings** sets the Mesh, Textures and Displace settings, the parts, options and pose it was made with again, to start a new one from.
  - **⋯** renames it, shows its folder, or deletes it.
- Variations are kept beside the model's package: `<game> packages/<path in the game>.darkpkg/variations/`. That works before the game is imported too, and importing it later (or again) keeps them, so they stay when the game is uninstalled.
- A number typed into a setting no longer comes back when that setting is changed some other way (by **Use its settings**, say).

This is the fourth stage of the model-package plan. Next: configurable models (weapons with parts and skins) as packages, then the whole asset store.

Tested on synthetic Dark Souls III files: a displaced weapon saved, replaced by another Generate, shown again with the same mesh, exported, its settings chosen again, renamed and deleted; and a variation kept through importing the game as packages and removing it.

## Windows
- **`DarkSTL_0.30.0_x64-setup.exe`** installs Dark STL for your account (no administrator rights). It isn't signed, so SmartScreen asks once: *More info > Run anyway*.
- **`DarkSTL_0.30.0_x64-portable.zip`** is the same app in a folder: unzip it anywhere and run `Dark STL.exe`.
