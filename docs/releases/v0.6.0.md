Generating a model is now three tabs that each do one thing and keep their result: **Mesh** evens out the vertices, **Textures** cleans up and upsamples the normal maps, with before and after to zoom into, and **Displace** raises the texture detail on the evened-out mesh. Change one and run it again without redoing the others.

## What changed
- **Mesh tab.** Step 1 on its own: the vertex count (with the spacing it gives on this model), the method (adaptive subdivision or isotropic remeshing, which decimates below the model's own count) and *Round off curved surfaces*. *Even out the vertices* makes the mesh and shows it in the 3D view as the Enhanced model, not displaced yet, and keeps it (*Done: N vertices… Kept while you work on the textures and the displacement*). The last two meshes are kept.
- **Textures tab: upscale for displacement.** The compression clean-up (none, smooth, or an AI model), upsampling (off, guided by the colour texture, or an AI model) and the factor (automatic for the Mesh tab's vertex count, 2× or 4×) moved here from Generate. *Upscale N maps* makes the normal maps Displace will read, with the settings as they are, and lists each with what was done to it; change a setting and upscale again as often as you like. Below the list, **Before / After** shows a map the way the engine will read it (as a normal map again): drag the line to compare, scroll or use − + to zoom down to single texels, drag to pan, or show only Before or only After. Each view is drawn by the engine at the pixels shown, so a 4096 px map zooms as quickly as a small one. The last 8 upscaled maps are kept (up to 768 MB).
- **Displace tab.** Step 2 on its own: where the detail comes from (normal maps, height maps, or nothing), the strength, a detail texture and strength for each material, and the finer settings (green channel, detail radius, slope integration, edge keeping, colour-texture detail, detail normal maps, the displacement limit). A list above the button says whether step 1's mesh is kept and how many of the normal maps are ready; whatever isn't is made first, with the Mesh and Textures tabs' settings, so *Displace* always works. The result (vertices, size, relief, what each material's texture went through) stays on this tab.
- **Stop is always shown.** While a job runs, the status bar shows a red **Stop** beside it, not only when the mouse is over it; each tab has its own Stop under its progress bar too.
- **Engine.** `texture_maps`, `texture_upscale` and `texture_crop` commands serve the Textures tab; `enhance` takes `step: "mesh"` for step 1 alone and reuses upscaled maps. The `upsample_factor` setting (0 automatic, 2 or 4) is new; the others are as before, so saved settings carry over.

## Windows
- **`DarkSTL_0.6.0_x64-setup.exe`** installs Dark STL for your account (no administrator rights). It isn't signed, so SmartScreen asks once: *More info > Run anyway*.
- **`DarkSTL_0.6.0_x64-portable.zip`** is the same app in a folder: unzip it anywhere and run `Dark STL.exe`.
