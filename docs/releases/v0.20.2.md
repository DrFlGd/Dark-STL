**Cyberpunk weapons get a card from their names when their appearance files give none**, and the app says what it found.

## What changed
- **Weapon cards from the game's naming.** Cyberpunk names a weapon's meshes `w_revolver__darra_nova__base1`, `w_revolver__darra_nova__mag1`, `w_revolver__darra_nova__scope1`... and keeps them all in one archive. The name guessing (0.15.0) grouped by folder and skipped folders with more than 48 files, and didn't take `base1` for a base, so no Cyberpunk weapon got a card that way. Meshes are now grouped by the name before the double underscore wherever they sit, `base1`/`body2` count as bases, and each base of a weapon (`base1`, `base2`) gets its own card sharing the parts. These cards say *Guessed*; a weapon whose appearance file (`.app`) is read keeps the card made from it (0.20.0).
- **What the appearance files gave.** The Home page's note for a Cyberpunk folder now says how many weapon appearance files were read, how many weapons and attachments they named, how many became configurable, and the first error if any; the engine log (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\DarkSTL\cache\darkstl.log`) has the details.
- **Collect keeps a weapon configurable.** Collecting a weapon from the archives now copies its `.app` and `.ent` beside its mesh, materials, layer setups, masks and textures, so the collected folder gives the same card (and a collected weapon is a complete sample to send along).

## Windows
- **`DarkSTL_0.20.2_x64-setup.exe`** installs Dark STL for your account (no administrator rights). It isn't signed, so SmartScreen asks once: *More info > Run anyway*.
- **`DarkSTL_0.20.2_x64-portable.zip`** is the same app in a folder: unzip it anywhere and run `Dark STL.exe`.
