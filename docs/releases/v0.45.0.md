**A Clean tab takes off what shouldn't be printed (hidden parts, thin cloth, faces you pick) and sets the print size, before the Mesh step.**

## What changed
- **A Clean tab, first in the Edit group.** Everything you take off the model, and how it stands, goes in a list of steps for that model. Each step shows what it did ("removed 1 part, 108 faces") and has a button that takes it away again, so nothing is lost. The list is done again every time the model is built, with the parts, options and pose you chose, and Mesh, Displace, Finish, Cut and Export all work on the cleaned model. The list is kept for each model, and saved variations keep the list they were made with (*Use its settings* brings it back).
- **Thin cloth, even inside a bigger part.** *Find thin sheets* lists the capes, linings and cloth that have little or no thickness, biggest first: open single layers, two layers drawn back to back, and layers thinner than the thickness you set. They're found even where the cloth isn't a separate part. Point at one in the list to see it in the 3D view; tick the ones to take off and remove them in one step.
- **Pick and remove.** Turn on *Pick faces* and click the model: the pick spreads over the surface until it meets a crease sharper than the angle you set. Shift+click adds to it, Alt+click takes away; the picked faces show in orange, and *Remove picked faces* takes them off.
- **Parts hidden inside others.** The part checkboxes moved from the Model tab to Clean (the Model tab still lists the parts, with a link). *Remove parts hidden inside others* finds whole pieces that sit inside another part (a body under armour, a lining inside a cuirass). *Find hidden faces* shows the surfaces buried deeper than a depth you choose inside other parts, before you remove them.
- **Repairs.** Fill small holes (up to a size across), drop duplicate faces of two-sided sheets, weld vertices closer than a distance, and turn faces that face the wrong way.
- **Print size and placement.** Set a print height in millimetres (or a scale), turn the model a quarter turn or by any angle, mirror it, move it, and stand it on the floor. From then on the size under the 3D view, the Model tab, the Textures and Displace readouts and Export are in print millimetres, with the game's own size shown faintly beside them.
- **Command line.** `darkstl export` and `darkstl enhance` take `--clean` steps, for example `--clean hidden-parts --clean sheets:0.5 --clean height:150`.

Tested on synthetic models built byte by byte: every step on its own and in a list, faces found again with other parts left out, the sheets list and its highlighting, click picking with Shift and Alt, the steps kept after the page is opened again, and the enhanced model and the exported STL made from the cleaned model at 150 mm tall.
