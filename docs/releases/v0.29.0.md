**Your own names and folders for a game's models, and models combined into one.**

## What changed
- **Rename or move…** (in a model's right-click menu, its summary and its page's ⋯ menu) gives a model a name of your own, such as its name in the game, and files it in a folder of your own: type `Kind/Group`, such as `Bosses` or `Weapons/Long swords`. A new name makes a new folder. The model's summary shows where the game's files put it, and **Go back to the game's name and place** undoes it.
- **Rename or move this folder…** in a kind's or group's summary renames it, or moves it into another, for every model in it: "Characters" becomes "Bosses". Models you moved there by hand go with it.
- **Combine with other models…** (in the same menus) makes one model of several, such as a character's face, body and hair. Search the game's models, tick the ones to add, and give the result a name and a folder. It is listed and opened like any other model; its Configure tab turns a part off or adds another.
- All of this is kept in Dark STL's settings, in each game's **library overlay** (`overlays/<game>.json`) and your configurations, and never changes the game's files or the packages. Models are named by their path in the game, so your names, folders and combined models hold through rescans, through importing the game as packages, and once the game is uninstalled.
- Renaming or moving a model doesn't redo anything you generated for it.

This is the third stage of the model-package plan. Next: saved variations of generated models, kept beside the model.

Tested on synthetic Dark Souls III files: a weapon renamed and moved into a new group, the characters renamed as a whole, a character combined with a weapon, kept through a restart and a rescan, then imported as packages and opened with the game removed.

## Windows
- **`DarkSTL_0.29.0_x64-setup.exe`** installs Dark STL for your account (no administrator rights). It isn't signed, so SmartScreen asks once: *More info > Run anyway*.
- **`DarkSTL_0.29.0_x64-portable.zip`** is the same app in a folder: unzip it anywhere and run `Dark STL.exe`.
