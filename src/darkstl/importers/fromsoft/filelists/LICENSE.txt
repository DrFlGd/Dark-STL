File lists for unpacking FromSoftware archives
==============================================

The game archives only store a hash of each file's path, so the names come from lists the
modding community put together (TKGP's UXM, then Nordgaren's UXM Selective Unpacker). The
lists here (*.txt.gz, one path per line, `#archive` section lines) are the copies Smithbox
ships, taken unchanged from https://github.com/vawser/Smithbox at commit
831193d1294586688adbdc288a01cb5fcb94b91b (src/Andre/Andre.Formats/Resources/*Dictionary.txt),
under Smithbox's licence below.

Settings > Game archives can download newer lists (UXM Selective Unpacker's, which also name
Elden Ring's Shadow of the Erdtree files) into the app's settings folder; they are read
together with these.
